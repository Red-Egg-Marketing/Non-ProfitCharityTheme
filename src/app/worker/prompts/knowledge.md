## Who MHBHC serves

Adults and adolescents in the Denver metro area, Sheridan, Aurora, and Summit County, including: women, men, Veterans, LGBTQ2S+ individuals, transgender and gender-expansive individuals, single-parent families, people living with HIV, youth, people experiencing homelessness, youth living in motels along Colfax in Aurora, runaway youth, and individuals returning from incarceration. Services address mental health, substance use, co-occurring disorders, trauma, and housing instability.

Everyone is welcome. MHBHC does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, gender expression, age, national origin, disability, marital status, sexual orientation, or military status.

## Programs

### Behavioral Health (outpatient) — Denver, Sheridan, Summit County

Individual, family, and group counseling; case management; treatment; relapse prevention; recovery services including risk reduction, healthy relationship training, life skills coaching, DUI education, and therapy. Assessment and referral for substance use and mental health. Peer support specialists. Aftercare through Transcend. Groups run days, evenings, and Saturdays; online and in-person.
→ Call (303) 825-8113

### Court 2 Community (C2C) — justice-involved adults

For people in the criminal justice system with substance use or mental health challenges. Team works routinely with probation and parole officers and treatment courts.

- Intensive outpatient, enhanced outpatient, and weekly outpatient programs
- Mental health and substance use evaluations for court/probation requirements
- Individual therapy, case management, peer support, abstinence monitoring
- **The Huddle Program** — whole-person reentry support after incarceration or specialty court: case management, employment and housing navigation, medical case management, vocational rehab, MAT, LGBTQ2S+-affirming services, transportation to treatment, peer recovery support
- **The RISE Program** — intensive outpatient program inside Denver County Jail for men and women with co-occurring disorders, 7am–4pm Mon–Sat; begins during incarceration and continues after release
- **The TECH Project** (Treatment • Empowerment • Community • Help) — for 18–30 year olds after release: pre- and post-release care, peer support, housing/health/job readiness resources, the Connections app from CHESS Health, sober community events. Offered at Sheridan Tue–Thu 11am–7pm, Fri 9am–5pm, Sat 8am–1pm
- **Momentum** — recovery support program for men at Sheridan: CBT, DBT, anger management, healthy relationship skills, trauma-informed care, peer support; same hours as TECH
  → Call (303) 825-8113 (Denver) or (303) 761-0200 (Sheridan)

### DUI Treatment Services

Level II Education, Level II Therapy, Level II 4+ Treatment, Interlock Enhancement Counseling. Available at Denver, Sheridan, and Summit County. Self-pay options available online at the Pay for Treatment page (DUI Education, DUI Therapy, DUI book, individual therapy, group therapy, MRT book).
→ Call (303) 825-8113

### Health Care clinic (physician assistant) — Denver

For current MHBHC clients only. Adults and adolescents.

- General primary care, physical exams, wellness checks, basic wound care
- STI testing and treatment; PrEP resources, prescriptions, and follow-up
- Gender-affirming care including hormone replacement therapy and referrals for gender-affirming procedures
- Medication Assisted Treatment (MAT)
- Medium-complexity mental health and substance use medications; bridge medications
- Birth control; public health education
  → Schedule: (303) 825-8113 or MEdapt@mhbhc.org · Refills: (303) 825-8113 x199

### Integrated Care — Sheridan & Summit County

Behavioral health and primary care in one location, in partnership with Porch Light Health / Front Range Clinic (Summit County also partners with Summit Wellness Hub). Nurse practitioners trained in addiction treatment, supervised by a board-certified addiction physician. Services: DUI education and therapy, outpatient programs, individual and group therapy, day/evening/Saturday classes, peer recovery support, abstinence monitoring (random breathalyzer and UA), crisis support, trauma support, MAT, case management, LGBTQ2S+-affirming services, referral/employment/housing support. Summit County also offers HIV/STI testing and SAP services (book at calendly.com/rfitch-mhbhc or RFitch@mhbhc.org).
→ Sheridan (303) 761-0200 · Summit (970) 485-6676

### Medication Assisted Treatment (MAT)

Evidence-based treatment combining medication with counseling and behavioral therapy for substance use. Medications used include Antabuse, Buprenorphine/Suboxone, Campral, and Vivitrol. Available at Sheridan and Summit County via Porch Light Health providers, and at the Denver health care clinic.
→ (303) 761-0200 (Sheridan) · (970) 485-6676 (Summit) · info@mhbhc.org

### Mind Lift Ketamine Clinic — Sheridan

Ketamine-assisted therapy for depression, anxiety, and PTSD, especially when other treatments haven't worked. Intramuscular ketamine sessions on Tuesdays and Fridays with therapist and nurse present; integration therapy sessions on Mondays and Thursdays; 2–3 preparation sessions first. Medicaid and fee arrangements accepted.
Requirements: medical referral approved by MHBHC provider; at least 90 days of sobriety; 18+; commitment to six ketamine sessions plus integration follow-ups; own transportation to and from appointments.
Not suitable for people with uncontrolled hypertension, history of psychosis, cardiovascular disease, prior adverse reaction to ketamine, or who are pregnant. Eligibility is determined by the clinical team.
→ KetamineApt@mhbhc.org · (303) 761-0200 · 3460 S. Federal Blvd., Sheridan

### Peer Support & Transcend Aftercare

MHBHC uses a fully peer-coach-driven, technology-integrated model. Every client is paired with a peer specialist on entry and stays with them through treatment, then moves into the Transcend aftercare program. The eRecovery / Connections app keeps clients connected with peers and clinicians, sends check-ins and reminders, and alerts peers when a client is near high-risk locations.

- **Transcend Recovery Support Warm Line: (720) 256-6525** — for current MHBHC clients only. Skill-building support calls, not a crisis line; texting also available. In an emergency call 911.
- mhbhc-transcend.org

### Prevention

- **Keepin' It REAL** — youth 12–17. Culturally grounded program reducing tobacco, alcohol, and drug use; teaches Refuse, Explain, Avoid, Leave.
- **PRIME for Life®** — ages 18–26. Motivational program for informed, low-risk choices about alcohol and drugs.
- Brochures available in English and Spanish on the Prevention page.
- Harm reduction education: Bee the Vibe (beethevibe.org)
- Knowledge is Freedom (knowingisfreedom.com)

### Housing Support Team (HoST) / Comitis Crisis Center — Aurora

Transitional shelter for VA-eligible families and single women; family shelter for TANF-eligible families; emergency overnight shelter; cold-weather activation; cooling centers during severe heat; behavioral health care for people accessing shelter. Family warming center offers a warm meal, hot shower, and safe place away from the elements. Also operates the Aurora Day Resource Center.
→ (303) 341-9160 · 2178 Victor St., Aurora · Text WARMUP to 313131 · comitiscrisiscenter.org
